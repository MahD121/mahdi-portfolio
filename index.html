Mahdi Jafari is an architect, BIM developer, and computational designer with over five years of experience across all project phases, from historic masonry to large-scale infrastructure, holding an MSc in Integrative Technologies from Universität Stuttgart.

His practice combines Open BIM methodology, parametric computation, and digital fabrication to make interdisciplinary projects more precise, efficient, and future-ready.

"Architecture begins with a point, a single coordinate in space. Everything else is the discipline of connecting them."

Bridging architectural intent and machine-readable data, Mahdi optimises BIM workflows across international teams: from IFC schema authoring and Revit API development to robotic fabrication and additive manufacturing.
